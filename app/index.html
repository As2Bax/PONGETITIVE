CONTROLS
  • Move: mouse, W/S, or Up/Down arrows.
  • Smash: left-click just before contact.
  • Pause: P / Esc. Mute: M.
CATCH ZONE
  1. Collect the ◎ power-up. Catch replaces smash for both sides for 30 seconds.
  2. Left-click just before contact to arm a 0.45-second catch.
  3. Once caught, aim with mouse or movement keys. Your paddle stays still.
  4. Left-click again to fire. The side bar shows charge; the arrow shows direction.

Full charge takes 1.2 seconds. Auto-release after 3 seconds. AI uses timed catches and aims too.

POWER-UPS

Hit a pickup with the ball. The last side to touch the ball claims it.

  • Big paddle: enlarges your paddle.
  • Shrink foe: reduces the opponent's paddle.
  • Multiball / Ball storm: adds one / up to seven balls.
  • Slow-mo: slows all balls.
  • Ghost paddle: adds an AI helper on your side.
  • Catch Zone: enables catching, aiming, and charged shots.
  • Flip: a 3-second warning, then the arena swaps sides for 10 seconds. The final 3 seconds count down before switching back. Scores and controls stay yours. Extra Flip pickups do not restart it.
BALL TYPES
  • Gold: 3 points.
  • Phantom: 2 points; fades in and out.
  • Comet: 2 points; smaller and faster.
  • Heavy: slower, heavier impacts.
  • Splitter: splits on its first paddle hit.
  • Fireball: high-speed balls ignite for at least 2 points.
FIELD EVENTS
  • Portals: teleport balls.
  • Gravity wells: bend ball paths.
  • Wind: pushes balls vertically.
  • Combo: long rallies raise the speed cap.
DIFFICULTY
  • Relaxed: bigger paddle, slower ball, gentler solo AI.
  • Easy / Normal: standard play; Easy gives more room to react.
  • Hard: smaller paddle, faster ball, paddle wear and fog.
  • Insane: stronger handicaps and a razor-sharp AI that smashes often and turns wells and portals against you.
  • Impossible: adds perfect AI, paddle flicker and inverted controls.
  • Custom: choose physics, AI skill, curses, balls, power-ups and events. Barrier adds closing arena walls.
  • Higher tiers also unlock more of the arena: field events and the trickier power-ups only appear as difficulty rises.
PLAYER VS PLAYER
  • Pick Player vs Player under Opponent, then create a room or join one with a code.
  • The host owns the match settings and starts the game; the guest's options stay locked.
  • Both players always see themselves on the right.
  • Pausing only opens the menu. The match keeps running for the other player.
